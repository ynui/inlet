Three small steps

From local to shareable.

Inlet forwards HTTP traffic to a port on your machine. Your app stays where it is.

01

Install the client

Use Homebrew or download a binary for your platform.

brew tap ynui/tap && brew install inlet
All downloads ↗Source ↗
02

Create your account

Open signup is enabled on the public demo. Release clients connect here by default; source builds use localhost.

inlet signup
03

Start your tunnel

Run your local app, then point inlet at its HTTP port.

inlet http 3000
Open signup is enabled on this public demo. Your token is saved on your machine after signup. The public URL forwards to your local app, so keep the app’s own authentication enabled when needed.
Under the hood

A direct path to your local app.

Your client opens one secure WebSocket connection to this server. Requests for your stable tunnel URL travel through that connection to the HTTP app on your machine.

Inlet handles the path and streams request and response bodies. Your app does not need to be deployed or reachable from the public internet directly.

One stable link

Your tunnel ID stays the same when the client reconnects. Rotate it when you want a new link.

Your token controls the tunnel

Invite codes are single-use. A claimed token is stored locally with restrictive file permissions.

HTTP apps

Inlet forwards HTTP. WebSocket upgrades from the public app URL are not currently supported.

Ready when you are

Bring your app to the edge.

Install the client, get an invite from the server operator, and share a link to the app already running on your machine.
